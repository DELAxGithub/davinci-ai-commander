## Description (Gumroad product page)

### DaVinci AI Commander

Type what you want in plain English. AI generates the code. DaVinci Resolve executes it.

**Stop clicking through menus for repetitive tasks.**

- "Create bins case01 to case30" → Done in 2 seconds
- "Set all clip colors to blue" → 47 clips updated
- "Render all timelines with YouTube preset" → 5 jobs queued

### What it automates

- **Bin management** — Create, rename, organize bins in bulk
- **Batch rendering** — Queue renders across multiple timelines
- **Markers** — Add, list, remove markers with notes
- **Metadata** — Read and write clip metadata
- **Clip properties** — Change colors, flags

### What it does NOT do

This tool handles structural operations only. It cannot cut, trim, apply effects, color grade, or modify your video content in any way. Your creative work stays untouched.

### Requirements

- macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later
- DaVinci Resolve 19+ (Free or Studio)
- Google Gemini API key (free tier available at aistudio.google.com)

### What's included

- DaVinci AI Commander.app (signed & notarized by Apple)
- Setup guide with screenshots
