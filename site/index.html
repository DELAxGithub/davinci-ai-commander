FAQ

Does it work with the free version of DaVinci Resolve?

Yes. Both Free and Studio editions are supported.

Do I need a Gemini API key?

Yes. You can get one for free at Google AI Studio. The free tier is generous enough for typical use.

Can it edit my video?

No. It automates structural tasks (bins, timelines, markers, rendering) via the Resolve Scripting API. It cannot cut, trim, apply effects, or color grade.

Is my data sent to the cloud?

Your commands are sent to Google Gemini to generate code. No video data or project files are uploaded.
